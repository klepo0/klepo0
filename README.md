Crisan-Pardoux &lpar;2024&rpar; address this classical problem with BSPDE techniques, Du et al. &lpar;2013&rpar;, a Sobolev-based approach that however requires increasingly strong regularity assumptions in high dimensions. Importantly, the rough filtering equations are seen, upon randomization, to coincide with the classical filtering equations. Our framework yields well-posedness &lpar;existence, uniqueness, stability&rpar; under dimension-independent regularity assumptions, providing a robust and conceptually unified solution to a longstanding problem in stochastic filtering theory.
